## Lobbies, rooms and chat
A lobby is where open rooms and chat live. Any Retro-Netplay app can run one; whoever runs it sets its name and rules and moderates it. In a lobby you can open a room for a game you're playing (others ask to join, and you let them in or turn on auto-accept), join other people's rooms, and chat with everyone in the lobby or just your room.

- Chat is for people 13 and older. Don't post hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, personal information about anyone, spam or links meant to scam or harm. Don't pretend to be someone else.
- Anyone can block you (they stop seeing your messages and you can't join their rooms) and report you to whoever runs the lobby. A room's host can remove you from the room; the person running the lobby can mute, remove or ban you.
- The swear-word filter hides common bad words on your device; it doesn't catch everything. Leave, block and report anyone who bothers you.
- Only open rooms for games you own and are playing. In a stream room the other players see and control your game; in a netplay room each player needs their own copy.

## Running a lobby
If you run a lobby that other people join, you are responsible for it: publish its name and rules (in `lobby.json` in the lobby's data folder; players see them when they join), read the reports it receives (they arrive in your app and are kept in `lobby-reports.jsonl` in its data folder), and mute, remove or ban people who break the rules. Bans are kept in `lobby-bans.json`. You can add your own words to hide in chat in `lobby-words.txt`. Respond to complaints, and follow the law where you live, which may include duties to act on illegal content you're told about. Don't keep chat or reports longer than you need them.

## Playing with others

Friends' privileges (join anytime, watch, pick a controller slot) let those friends join, watch or take a controller in your games without asking each time. Only give them to people you trust, and remove them (or the friend) whenever you like. Choose a name that isn't offensive and doesn't pretend to be someone else.
