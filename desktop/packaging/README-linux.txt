RETRO-NETPLAY FOR LINUX

Start it: double-click "Retro-Netplay" in this folder.
(If your file manager opens it as text instead, right-click it > Run, or in a terminal: ./Retro-Netplay)

The first time, a short setup asks for your game folders, finds your emulators, opens the netplay port
on your router (you can say no) and can add Retro-Netplay to your app menu.

Nothing to install: Python and everything else the app needs is inside this folder.
Emulators are separate: install RetroArch (and Dolphin / PCSX2 / RPCS3 if you use them) from your app store.

Hosting PS2/PS3 streams for a friend also needs GStreamer on your computer. In the app's setup check it
says exactly what to install.
