RETRO-NETPLAY FOR LINUX

Start it: double-click "retro-netplay" in this folder.
(If your file manager opens it as text instead, right-click it > Run, or in a terminal: ./retro-netplay)

The first time, pick a player name and confirm you're 13 or older, then add the folder where your games are
(My games > Add a folder). README.txt in the project explains rooms, lobbies and chat step by step.

Nothing to install: the app's window, its engine (Python) and the player window are inside this folder.
Emulators are separate: install RetroArch (and Dolphin / PCSX2 / RPCS3 if you use them) from your app store.

Hosting stream rooms also needs GStreamer on your computer (Settings shows what's missing when you host).
